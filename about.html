Yu Ong Ching

Deputy Special Officer to the Prime Minister of Malaysia. Lead Digital Strategist at DAP Headquarters for over a decade. Founder of Ubah.TV — Malaysia's first live political broadcast platform, launched in 2013 before Facebook supported native live streaming.

I write at the intersection of data, political communication, and governance. My analysis draws on first-hand experience designing national narrative strategies for three General Elections (GE13, GE14, GE15), and on quantitative methods from my MPA at Tsinghua University.

Currently a PhD candidate at ISSCAD, Peking University, researching agency dynamics in Malaysia's digital public sphere.
